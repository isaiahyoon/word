Reading type for Gagana Sāmoa

Twenty-two serif families set with the same Samoan title and orthographic marks. Compare warmth, long-line rhythm, and the clarity of macrons and the glottal stop. These are reading candidates, not claims that a typeface is inherently Samoan.
