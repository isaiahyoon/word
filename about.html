OUR CONVICTION

The text comes first.

The King James Version is the canonical foundation of this project. Other editions are offered as translations for reading and comparison; they do not replace or redefine that foundation.

Reading is free, with no account and no advertisements. Search happens on your device. Your reading place and saved editions stay in your browser, and offline reading is there when you need it.

TAKE IT WITH YOU

Install The Amazing Word.

Use your browser's install or add-to-home-screen option. Once installed, the reader opens in its own window.

Choose your default reading language

Choose a language, then continue. To change it later, switch editions in the reader; that edition becomes your default in that app or browser.

Mac

In Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.

iPhone

In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Windows

In Edge, choose Settings and more → Apps → Install this site as an app. Chrome offers Install page as app in its menu.

Browser menu names can vary. To read offline, open the Reading Edition panel in the reader and save each edition you would like to keep.
